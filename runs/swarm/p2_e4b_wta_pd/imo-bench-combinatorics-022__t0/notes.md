### [W2, round 1]
**Lattice Setup and Hexagon Conditions**

**1. Coordinate System:**
We define an oblique coordinate system $(m, n)$ based on two unit lattice vectors, $\mathbf{a}_1$ and $\mathbf{a}_2$, where the angle between them is $60^\circ$.
Let $\mathbf{a}_1$ lie along the x-axis: $\mathbf{a}_1 = (1, 0)$ in Cartesian coordinates.
Let $\mathbf{a}_2$ be at $60^\circ$: $\mathbf{a}_2 = (1/2, \sqrt{3}/2)$.
Any lattice vertex $V$ has coordinates $(m, n)$ and corresponds to the vector position:
$$V = m\mathbf{a}_1 + n\mathbf{a}_2$$

**2. Distance Formula:**
The squared distance $S^2$ between two lattice points $V_A = (m_A, n_A)$ and $V_B = (m_B, n_B)$ is given by:
$$S^2 = (m_A - m_B)^2 + (n_A - n_B)^2 + (m_A - m_B)(n_A - n_B)$$
Let $\Delta m = m_A - m_B$ and $\Delta n = n_A - n_B$. Then $S^2 = (\Delta m)^2 + (\Delta n)^2 + \Delta m \Delta n$.

**3. Conditions for a Regular Hexagon:**
A regular hexagon with side length $S$ whose vertices $V_0, V_1, \dots, V_5$ lie on the lattice must satisfy two conditions:

*   **Equal Side Lengths:** The distance between consecutive vertices must be constant:
    $$S^2 = \text{Dist}^2(V_k, V_{k+1}) \quad \text{for } k=0, \dots, 5 \quad (V_6 = V_0)$$

*   **$60^\circ$ Rotational Symmetry (Vector Condition):** The displacement vectors defining the sides, $\mathbf{s}_k = V_{k+1} - V_k$, must be separated by $60^\circ$. In the oblique lattice coordinates, if a side vector is defined by $\mathbf{s}_k = (\Delta m_k, \Delta n_k)$, the vector for the next side, $\mathbf{s}_{k+1}$, must correspond to a specific lattice vector rotation. This means the six displacement vectors must be cyclic permutations related to three fundamental vectors $\mathbf{v}_A, \mathbf{v}_B, \mathbf{v}_C$, such that $\mathbf{v}_A + \mathbf{v}_B + \mathbf{v}_C = \mathbf{0}$ (or combinations thereof).

A simplified algebraic condition arises when considering the vectors $\mathbf{s}_k$ themselves: they must be the basis vectors of a lattice polygon that is centrally symmetric and has $D_6$ symmetry. If the side length vector is $V_1 - V_0 = (m_1, n_1)$, the subsequent vectors must cyclically relate to $(m_1, n_1)$.

Specifically, for a regular hexagon with side length $S$ corresponding to integer components $(\Delta m, \Delta n)$, the six displacement vectors $(\Delta m_k, \Delta n_k)$ must be of the form:
$$(\Delta m_k, \Delta n_k) \in \{ (m, n), (n, m+n), (-m-n, m), (-m, -n), (-n, -m-n), (m+n, -m) \}$$
(or cyclic permutations thereof, depending on orientation).

Thus, the required algebraic conditions are:
1. All six consecutive displacement vectors must be chosen from a set of 6 vectors defined by $(m, n)$ and its $60^\circ$ lattice rotations (as listed above).
2. The vector $\mathbf{v} = (m, n)$ must satisfy $m^2 + n^2 + mn = S^2$, where $S^2$ is a perfect square in terms of the lattice step size, although $S$ itself is not necessarily an integer.
3. The sum of these six displacement vectors must be zero (guaranteed if they are chosen as the 6 symmetric neighbors of a central vector).
