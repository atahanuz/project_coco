$$m \binom{n}{k} = 2m \binom{n-1}{k-1}$$

$$\binom{n}{k} = 2 \binom{n-1}{k-1}$$
Expanding the binomial coefficients:
$$\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!} = 2 \frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!(n-k)!}$$
We use the properties $n! = n \cdot (n-1)!$ and $k! = k \cdot (k-1)!$:
$$\frac{n \cdot (n-1)!}{k \cdot (k-1)!(n-k)!} = 2 \frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!(n-k)!}$$
By canceling common terms $\frac{(n-1)!}{(k-1)!(n-k)!}$ (assuming $n>k \ge 1$):
$$\frac{n}{k} = 2$$
$$n = 2k$$
