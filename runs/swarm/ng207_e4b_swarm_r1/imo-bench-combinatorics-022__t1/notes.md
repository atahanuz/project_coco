### Analysis of Lattice Structure and Constraints (W2 Task)

**1. Coordinate System Definition:**
The triangular grid is defined by unit equilateral triangles. We establish an oblique coordinate system $(a, b)$ with basis vectors $\mathbf{u}$ and $\mathbf{v}$:
$\mathbf{u} = (1, 0)$
$\mathbf{v} = (1/2, \sqrt{3}/2)$
A lattice point $P$ is represented by integer coordinates $(a, b) \in \mathbb{Z}^2$, such that $P = a\mathbf{u} + b\mathbf{v}$.

**2. Distance and Side Length Constraint ($k$):**
The squared distance $d^2$ between two lattice points $P_1=(a_1, b_1)$ and $P_2=(a_2, b_2)$ is:
$$d^2 = (a_1-a_2)^2 + (b_1-b_2)^2 + (a_1-a_2)(b_1-b_2)$$
For a regular hexagon of side length $k$ to exist with vertices on the lattice, $k^2$ must be representable in this form. Let $\Delta a = a_1-a_2$ and $\Delta b = b_1-b_2$.
The necessary algebraic condition for the side length $k$ is:
$$k^2 = A^2 + B^2 + AB \quad \text{for integers } A, B.$$

**3. Boundary Constraint (Within $H_L$):**
The large hexagon $H_L$ has side length $L=100$. When centered at the origin of the lattice coordinates (assuming standard alignment), $H_L$ contains all lattice points $(a, b)$ satisfying the inequalities derived from its sides:
$$\max(|a|, |b|, |a-b|) \le L$$

$$|A_0 + \Delta a_i| \le L$$
$$|B_0 + \Delta b_i| \le L$$
$$|A_0 + \Delta a_i - (B_0 + \Delta b_i)| \le L$$
