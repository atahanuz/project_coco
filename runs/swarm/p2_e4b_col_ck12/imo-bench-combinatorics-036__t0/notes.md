This is the standard graph distance in the Cayley graph of $S_N$ generated by adjacent transpositions.
2.  **Relation to Displacement:** The minimum swaps $n$ (the diameter) measures the *total* displacement of all elements (summed up as inversions), which is a stronger constraint than simply observing the maximum displacement of any single element.
3.  **Cyclic Constraint ($C_N$):** Since assignments that coincide under rotation are considered the same, we are analyzing the diameter of the quotient graph. The vertices are the orbits of $S_N$ under the action of the cyclic group $C_N$ (rotations).
4.  **Formal Formulation:** The smallest integer $n$ is the diameter of the graph where vertices are the rotational orbits of assignments.
    $$n = \text{Diam}\left(\text{Cayley}(S_N, \text{Adjacent Transpositions}) / C_N\right)$$
This means we seek the maximum minimum distance between any two orbits under the standard inversion metric.
