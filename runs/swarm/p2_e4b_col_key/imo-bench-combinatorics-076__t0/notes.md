**Argument against Sufficiency of N=17**

1.  **Local Density Requirement:** To generate the first $M$ Fibonacci numbers ($F_2, \ldots, F_M$) where $M$ is large, the set $S$ must contain many elements that are closely spaced (i.e., $\min(|x-y|)$ must be small). **Conclusion:** Based on the failure of simple structured sets to cover even small segments of the required sequence, and the severe conflicting demands of local density versus global sparsity imposed by $F_k$ and $L_{100}$, it is argued that $N=17$ is insufficient because no such highly structured set $S$ can be constructed.
