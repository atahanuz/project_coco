The maximum element in $A_{T+1}$ is determined by either the maximum element of $A_T$ or the maximum sum of adjacent elements in $A_T$.
$M_{T+1} = \max \left( M_T, \max_{k} \{A_T[k] + A_T[k+1]\} \right)$.
