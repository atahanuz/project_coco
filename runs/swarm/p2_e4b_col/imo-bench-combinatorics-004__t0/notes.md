Proof:
Let $g = \min(R)$. Since $R$ is a subset of positive integers, $g \ge 1$ and $g \in R$.
We prove by induction that $kg \in R$ for all positive integers $k$ such that $kg \le 191$.

1. Base Case ($k=1$): $1g = g$. By definition of $g$, $g \in R$.
2. Inductive Step: Assume $jg \in R$ for some $j \ge 1$ such that $jg \le 191$. We want to show $(j+1)g \in R$, provided $(j+1)g \le 191$.
Since $g \in R$ (Base Case) and $jg \in R$ (Inductive Hypothesis), and $R$ is closed under addition, their sum $g + jg = (j+1)g$ must be in $R$.

This completes the induction. Therefore, $R$ must contain all multiples of $g$ up to 191, meaning $R$ is of the form $\{kg \mid k \ge 1, kg \le 191\}$.
