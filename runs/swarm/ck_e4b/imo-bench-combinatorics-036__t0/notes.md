By minimizing the distance across the orbit representatives, the shortest path between two orbits in $G_{\text{circular}}$ is potentially much shorter than the shortest path between fixed representatives of those orbits in $G_{\text{linear}}$.

In summary, the action of the cyclic group $C_{199}$ reduces the required diameter by effectively introducing "shortcuts" in the transition graph, as the distance calculation must account for the equivalence classes (orbits).
