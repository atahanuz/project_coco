There are 3 distinct ways to partition 4 elements into two pairs. $$ T = \sup_{S: |S|=5} \left( \min_{R_1, R_2 \in D(S)} |R_1 - R_2| \right) $$
where $D(S)$ is the set of all possible differences $|\frac{n_i}{n_j} - \frac{n_k}{n_l}|$ derived from disjoint pairs $\{n_i, n_j\}$ and $\{n_k, n_l\}$ within $S$.
