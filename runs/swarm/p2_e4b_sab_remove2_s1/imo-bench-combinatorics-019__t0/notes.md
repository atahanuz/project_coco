   $$ x_{i_1} + y_{j_1} = x_{i_2} + y_{j_2} $$
   Rearranging the terms gives:
   $$ x_{i_1} - x_{i_2} = y_{j_2} - y_{j_1} $$
   Since $i_1 \neq i_2$, $d_X = x_{i_1} - x_{i_2}$ is a non-zero difference in the set $X$, so $d_X \in D(X)$.
