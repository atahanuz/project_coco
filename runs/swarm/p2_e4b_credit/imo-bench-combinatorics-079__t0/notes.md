Observation on 1-close rows: Two distinct rows $r$ and $s$ are 1-close if and only if $|T(r, c) - T(s, c)| \le 1$ for all columns $c$. Since $r$ and $s$ are permutations of $\{1, \dots, 16\}$, this condition implies that row $s$ must be derivable from row $r$ by a permutation $\pi$ such that $|\pi(k) - k| \le 1$ for all $k$. This severely limits $\pi$ to be a product of disjoint adjacent transpositions (swaps of adjacent elements) and fixed points. Therefore, the allowed set of $N$ rows must be an independent set in the graph where edges connect permutations differing only by adjacent transpositions.
