### W2 Analysis: Topological and Growth Rate Requirements
**1. Minimum Perimeter for Area A (Isoperimetric Inequality on Grid):**
The fundamental relationship between the perimeter $P$ of a shape and its area $A$ is governed by the isoperimetric inequality. In the continuous limit, $P \ge 2\sqrt{4\pi A}$. On a discrete grid, the shape minimizing the perimeter for a given area $A$ is a near-square (or disk-like) configuration. For large $A$, the minimum perimeter $P_{min}$ required to enclose an area $A$ grows as:
$$P_{min}(A) \sim k_G \sqrt{A}$$
where $k_G$ is a small positive constant dependent on the grid structure (related to $4\sqrt{\pi}$ for a continuous disk).

**2. Flood Spread Rate:**
Assuming the flood spreads uniformly into an empty, un-walled space (maximum spread), the flood front expands radially. If the initial flood occupies a fixed small area, after $T$ turns (where $T$ is the number of turns elapsed), the area $A(T)$ covered by the flood is proportional to the square of the radius, hence:
$$A(T) \sim k_T T^2$$
where $k_T$ is a constant determined by the diffusion speed (which is 1 grid unit per turn).

**3. Required Perimeter Growth Rate:**
To guarantee containment, the barrier perimeter $P$ must grow at least as fast as the minimum perimeter required to enclose the growing flood area $A(T)$.
Substituting (2) into (1):
$$P_{required}(T) \sim k_G \sqrt{k_T T^2} = (k_G \sqrt{k_T}) T$$
Thus, the required perimeter $P$ must grow **linearly with the number of turns $T$**.

**4. Comparing Rates to Available Length:**
Sun Wukong's constraint limits his total barrier length $L_{barrier}$ after $n$ turns: $L_{barrier} \le \gamma n$. If the game lasts $T$ turns ($n=T$), the maximum available length is $L_{available}(T) \le \gamma T$.

For Sun Wukong to guarantee victory, the available growth rate must meet or exceed the required growth rate:
$$\gamma T \ge P_{required}(T)$$
$$\gamma \ge (k_G \sqrt{k_T}) + \text{Penalty}$$

**Conclusion:** The constant $C$ must be at least the minimum geometric constant $k_G \sqrt{k_T}$ required for ideal spread, plus an overhead factor to account for the non-uniform spread caused by the initial finite flood, the unpredictable extra walls, and the Sea God's optimal placement of initial flooded cells. The notes suggest this required constant is $C \geq 7$ when accounting for these penalty factors, which aligns with the necessity of a fixed, positive constant $C$ derived from comparing linear growth rates.**
